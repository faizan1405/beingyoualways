- Product gallery infographic slides are built by `build.py` from `src/content/infographics.json` with the shared template here (`shared/slide.css`) and exported to `src/assets/products/infographics/<slug>/` as 1600² WebP; text is never AI-rendered and packaging is the real photo — keeps copy correct and editable.
- Set status decides visibility: `ready` (client-verified directions) and `preview` (How to use is a conservative general guide, `how.guide: "general"`) reach shoppers via `isInfographicShown`; `draft` stays admin-only and the product keeps its previous gallery. General guides are flagged "pending client approval" in admin and on the slide, never promoted to `ready` without the client — keeps unverified directions honest.
- General guides never invent amounts, timings, frequency or dilution; numbers only when copied from the client's sheet/pack. If a safe method can't be established (e.g. wax preparation), the set stays `draft`.
