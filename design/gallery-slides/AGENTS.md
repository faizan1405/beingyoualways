- Product gallery infographic slides are built by `build.py` from `src/content/infographics.json` with the shared template here (`shared/slide.css`) and exported to `src/assets/products/infographics/<slug>/` as 1600² WebP; text is never AI-rendered and packaging is the real photo — keeps copy correct and editable.
- Set status decides visibility: `ready` reaches shoppers via `isInfographicShown`; `draft` stays admin-only and the product keeps its previous gallery (`preview` is a legacy alias of ready). `how.guide: "general"` marks a common category How to use (client-allowed where exact directions are missing): it keeps the "General guide · follow your pack" marker on the slide and a note in admin — keeps guides distinguishable from pack directions.
- Wording precedence: real pack photos → client sheet → original listing → common category guide; guides never invent amounts, timings, frequency or dilution beyond numbers the client supplied, and unproven pack claims (certifications) stay off.
- Slide 01 is `src/assets/products/gallery/<slug>/01-hero.webp` (real pack cut-out on white); `shared/packs/<slug>.png` is the cut-out reused inside slides. `photos` in the JSON records which client photos were used.
